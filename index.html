About

The Sound
of Somewhere
Else

SADUBAS are Ameet Mehta and Robin Sukhadia — childhood friends conjuring sonic hallucinations with tabla, beat machines, and Golden Era Bollywood samples since 1999.

Their music converges classical Indian rhythms with 70s Bollywood vibes to create psychedelic soundscapes that are one part trip hop and two parts cinematic South Asia — the soundtrack to a film that was never made, a world that almost was.

Performed at The Academy Museum of Motion Pictures, LACMA, the Hammer Museum, and the Bowers Museum.
